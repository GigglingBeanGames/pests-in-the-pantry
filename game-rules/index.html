01

Set the table

Your goal: empty both your hand and your Pantry.

  1. Make your Pantry

    Deal each player 4 face-down cards in a row. Don’t look at them.

  2. Deal starting hands

    2 players10
    3 players9
    4 players8
    5 players7
    6 players6
    7 players5

    Cards per player. The remaining cards become the face-down draw deck.

  3. Start the play pile

    Flip the top numbered card. If it’s Pest Control, flip another. The starting card’s power does not activate.

  4. Choose who starts

    Youngest player starts round 1. After that, the last round’s winner starts. Agree on up to 10 rounds.

02

Your turn, in a nutshell

01 / Play if you can

Beat the top card

  • A higher number, or
  • The same number in a stronger color.
  • Rat / 0 works on any non-zero. On a 0, it still needs a stronger color.
Green < Red < Blue < Gold

02 / Use its power

Play, then resolve

You must play a numbered Pest if you can. Singles, duplicates, and runs are allowed.

If your hand empties and Pantry cards remain, take 1 Pantry card first, then resolve the power.

Both empty? You win the round immediately. Your final card’s power does not activate.

03 / Can’t play?

Draw 1. End your turn.

If you have no numbered play, draw exactly 1 card.

You cannot play it immediately, even if it fits.

Pest Control is always optional. You may use it to pass when you have no numbered play.

03

Play more than one card

Duplicates · same number

777
  • Play multiple cards with the same number.
  • The group counts as its strongest color.
  • The power activates only once.

Runs · 3+ in one color

345
  • 3 or more consecutive numbers, all the same color. No gaps or duplicates.
  • The highest card must be legal on the pile. A run beginning with Rat / 0 can be played on any card.
  • Choose one legal numbered card in the run, put it on top, and activate its power.
  • The highest legal card you choose becomes the new top card.
04

Work through your Pantry

Four cards. Face down to start.

  • Ant / 1: move one Pantry card into your hand.
  • Fly / 4: reveal a face-down Pantry card and leave it there, or take a face-up Pantry card into your hand.

Empty hand? Take one.

While Pantry cards remain, immediately take 1 whenever your hand empties.

  • Choose a revealed card, or take any face-down card blindly.
  • Do this before the played card’s power.

Pest Control

Opt out or pass

Optional. No number. Avoid an effect or pass when you have no numbered play.

Can’t complete a power? The rest of the play still happens. For example, you can play Raccoon even when the Trash has no numbered cards.

06

An attack is coming. Now what?

Hornet +2 · Mosquito +1

Attacks add up as players pass them onward. The next player must respond.

Add onPlay a legal 5 or 6. Add its amount and pass the attack onward.
BlockPlay Beetle / 7 to stop the entire attack.
Use a runPlay a valid run containing 5, 6, or 7 and choose the appropriate power.
Opt outUse Pest Control. Take no cards; pass the full attack onward.
Take itNo numbered defense? Draw the full accumulated attack and lose your turn.

Two important exceptions

  • Beetle / 7 is always playable during an attack. A defensive run containing Beetle also works regardless of the top card.
  • Duplicates activate once. Multiple Hornets still add only 2; multiple Mosquitoes add only 1.

Block ≠ opt out. Beetle ends the attack. Pest Control sends it to the next player.

07

Pest Control · your escape hatch

4 cards · optional · not numbered

  • Opt out of a power affecting you.
  • Avoid an attack and pass it onward.
  • Opt out of a Termite exchange.
  • Pass when you have no numbered play.

Slide it into the pile. The top numbered card stays the same.

Know the limits

  • During Termite, you neither give nor receive a card. Other players pass around you.
  • You cannot combine Pest Control with other cards.
  • Raccoon cannot retrieve it.
  • It cannot be your final winning card.
08

Clear the pile. Keep playing.

Everyone else passed?

Once every other player has passed since the last successful play:

  1. Move the entire play pile to the Trash.
  2. The last player who played starts a new pile with any legal card from their hand.

Three other resets

  • Moth / 3: clear the pile immediately and play again.
  • Everyone passes on the original starting card: move it to the Trash, flip a new starter, and let the original starting player try again.
  • Draw deck runs out: shuffle the Trash into a new draw deck.
09

Empty both. Score the round.

1 + Pantry

Lowest total wins

Play the agreed number of rounds, up to 10. Add your points after each round.

The player with the fewest total points wins the game.
