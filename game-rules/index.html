Goal

Be the first player to get rid of every card in your hand and Pantry. The winner of each round scores 0 points. Every other player scores 1 point plus 1 point for each card left in their Pantry. Play an agreed number of rounds (up to 10). The player with the fewest points wins.

Setup

1. Give each player four face-down cards. Place them in a row without looking at them. This is their Pantry.
2. Deal each player a starting hand:

Starting hands: 2 players — 10 cards each; 3 — 9; 4 — 8; 5 — 7; 6 — 6; 7 — 5.

3. Place the remaining cards face down as the draw deck.
4. Flip the top numbered card to begin the play pile. If it is Pest Control, flip a new card. The starting card’s power does not activate.
5. Youngest player starts round 1. Afterwards, for the remainder of the game whoever won the last round starts the new round.

Taking a Turn

You must play a numbered Pest if you can.
You may play:
* A higher number than the top card.
* The same number in a stronger color.
* A Rat/0, which can be played on any card (if zero must be of a stronger color).
Colors rank from strongest to weakest:
Gold > Blue > Red > Green
A 0 played on another 0 must still have a stronger color.
If you cannot make a numbered play, draw one card and end your turn. You may not immediately play the card you drew.

Playing Multiple Cards

Duplicates
You may play multiple cards with the same number together.
* The group counts as the strongest color among the cards played.
* The card’s power activates only once.
Runs
You may play a run of three or more consecutive numbers in the same color.
* A run cannot contain duplicate numbers or gaps.
* The highest card in the run must be legal on the play pile.
* A run beginning with Rat/0 may be played on any card.
* Choose one numbered card in the run that can legally be played on the play pile and place it on top (such power will activate).
* The highest legal card you choose becomes the new top card.

Your Pantry

All four Pantry cards begin face down.
* Fly/4 lets you reveal one face-down Pantry card. It stays in the Pantry. However, a Fly/4 card also allows a face-up Pantry card to be brought to your hand.
* Ant/1 lets you move one Pantry card into your hand.
* Whenever you empty your hand while Pantry cards remain, immediately move one Pantry card into your hand.
* You may choose a revealed card, or choose any face-down card blindly.
* This Pantry transfer happens before the power of the card you just played activates.
You win immediately when both your hand and Pantry are empty. The final card’s power does not activate.

Pest Powers

If a power cannot be completed, the rest of the play still happens. For example, you may play Ant with no face-up Pantry cards or Raccoon when the Trash contains no numbered cards.

Hornet and Mosquito Attacks

Hornet/5 adds two cards to an attack. Mosquito/6 adds one.
The next player must respond by doing one of the following:
* Play a legal 5 or 6 to add its value to the attack and pass it onward.
* Play Beetle/7 to block the entire attack.
* Play a valid run containing 5, 6, or 7 and choose the appropriate power.
* Use Pest Control to avoid the attack and pass the full attack onward.
* If no numbered defense is available, draw the full accumulated attack and lose the turn.
Beetle/7 may always be played during an attack. A defensive run containing Beetle may also be played regardless of the current top card.
Duplicate attack cards still activate their power only once.

Pest Control (4 cards)

Pest Control is optional and is not a numbered card.
It may be used to:
* Opt out of a card effect that would affect you.
* Avoid and pass along an attack.
* Opt out of a Termite exchange.
* Pass your turn when you have no numbered play.
When used, slide Pest Control into the play pile without changing the top numbered card. A player who opts out of Termite neither gives nor receives a card; the other players pass around them.
Pest Control cannot be combined with other cards, recovered by Raccoon, or played as your final winning card.

Clearing and Rebuilding the Pile

When every other player has passed since the last successful play:
1. Move the entire play pile to the Trash.
2. The last player who successfully played begins a new pile with any legal card from their hand.
Moth/3 clears the pile immediately and allows its player to play again.
If everyone passes on the original starting card, move it to the Trash, flip a new starting card, and let the original starting player try again.
When the draw deck runs out, shuffle the Trash to form a new draw deck.

Winning

The first player to empty both their hand and Pantry wins the round and scores 0 points. Each other player scores 1 point plus 1 point for every card remaining in their Pantry. Continue until you have completed the agreed number of rounds (up to 10). The player with the fewest total points wins.
